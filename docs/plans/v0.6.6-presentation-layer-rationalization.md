## How to run the work — the process that was violated

The instructions in `AGENTS.md` were clear and were not followed. The six rules
added there (see *How to work here*) exist because of specific failures on this
branch:

1. **The plan is a file.** This file. Every turn that discovers work appends to
   it and shows the diff. Four lines of prose before a tool call is not planning
   — planning is maintaining the artifact so scope growth is visible without
   anyone having to ask.
2. **Read before acting, and cite what you read.** Restate the scope and name the
   document that defines it. The previous session read the supplied plan four
   hours in and retracted five findings as a result.
3. **A task closes on a committed check, not on a hand-run command.**
4. **Scope deltas go to the owner before they are built.** Discovering work is
   normal. Deciding unilaterally what to do with it is what produced this.
5. **Assert on which check fired, never on a substring of its output.**
6. **Run the adversarial reviewer per work item, before commit.** Every real
   defect on this branch was found by a reviewer, and they were run at the end,
   which is why the work took a day instead of an afternoon.
